# Higgsfield — de volledige prompt

Waar: higgsfield.ai → bovenbalk → **Image**. Niet Marketing Studio; die vraagt om
een product en dat heb je niet.

Wat je nooit laat genereren: je app-scherm. Een verzonnen scherm dat op VoxClip
lijkt is een bewering over je product. In elke prompt hieronder staat het scherm
weg-gedraaid of buiten beeld. Je echte screenshot plak je er in de montage overheen.

---

## De prompt — alles in één, plak dit

A candid, unposed photograph of a woman in her early thirties sitting at a plain
wooden desk in a quiet apartment on an ordinary weekday afternoon. She is in the
middle of working, not looking at the camera, one hand resting near the keyboard of
an open laptop, the other holding a pen above a paper notebook. Her posture is
relaxed and slightly asymmetric, the way someone sits when nobody is watching.
Shot on an iPhone held by a friend standing behind and to her right, so the framing
is a little off-centre and tilted by a few degrees. Natural light only: a large
window out of frame to the left throws soft directional daylight across the desk,
with gentle falloff into the shadows on the right side of the room. No flash, no
fill light, no studio equipment. The laptop screen is angled away from the camera
and is not readable — no interface, no text, no glow. Muted, desaturated palette:
warm off-white walls, a deep charcoal knit sweater, pale cream paper, honey-toned
wood. Exactly one saturated element in the entire frame — a small teal object on
the desk, a pen cap or a folded sticky note, no larger than a matchbox. Generous
empty space around her: bare wall above, clear desk surface in the foreground.
Fine natural sensor grain, true skin texture with visible pores and stray hairs,
slight motion softness in the hand. Colour like an unedited phone photo, not
graded. The mood is calm, quiet and unremarkable — a person mid-thought on a
Tuesday. Vertical 9:16 framing.

---

## Negatieve prompt — als er een apart veld voor is

app interface, UI mockup, software screenshot, readable screen, screen glow, text
overlay, captions, logos, brand marks, watermark, microphone, headset, podcast
setup, clipboard icon, wall of sticky notes, stock photo smile, looking at camera,
posed corporate portrait, studio lighting, ring light, rim light, teal or turquoise
background wash, colour gradient, bokeh lights, 3D render, illustration, cartoon,
oversaturated, HDR, beauty filter, plastic skin, airbrushed, extra fingers,
deformed hands, cluttered desk

Is er geen apart veld, laat hem dan weg. De prompt zelf houdt het meeste al tegen.

## Twee varianten, zelfde opbouw

**Het moment vóór de gedachte weg is** — vervang de eerste twee zinnen door:

A candid, unposed photograph of a man in his late twenties standing at a kitchen
counter with a coffee mug in one hand, looking slightly off-camera as if something
has just occurred to him. Morning light comes through a window behind him, soft
backlight with a faint haze. No screen of any kind in frame.

**Twee toetsen** — vervang de eerste twee zinnen door:

An extreme close-up photograph of two hands resting on a laptop keyboard, seen from
just above and slightly to the side. Only the hands and part of the keyboard are in
focus. The screen is entirely outside the frame.

## Na het genereren

Teal is `#12B3A6` en mag hooguit een tiende van het beeld zijn — één klein object,
nooit een achtergrond of een waas over alles. Komt er iets uit dat overal teal is:
weggooien en opnieuw. Ink is `#1C2230`, Paper is `#F7F7F5`.

Daarna: Studio → **Assets** → uploaden → bij *Where it came from* kies
**Model-generated**. Gegenereerd beeld mag nooit als screenshot de deur uit; de
Studio weigert dat ook.
