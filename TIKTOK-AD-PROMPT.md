# TikTok-ad voor VoxClip — de prompt

9:16, ongeveer 12 seconden, vier shots. Model: `kling3_0` (kan meerdere shots) of
`seedance_2_5` (algemener). Aspect ratio moet expliciet `9:16`.

**De regel:** geen app-scherm in beeld, en de persoon zegt niets over VoxClip. Zij
speelt het moment; de belofte komt van jouw tekst eroverheen. Een gegenereerd mens
die je product prijst is een verzonnen review, en de eerste die het narekent ziet
dat.

---

## De prompt

Vertical 9:16 short-form advertisement, four continuous shots, no on-screen text.

Shot 1, three seconds: a woman in her early thirties at a plain wooden desk in a
quiet apartment, filmed candidly on a phone from slightly behind her right
shoulder. She is scrolling back through something with growing impatience,
clicking, scrolling again, jaw tight. Her laptop screen is angled away from camera
and unreadable. Soft daylight from a window out of frame on the left.

Shot 2, three seconds: closer on her face, three-quarter profile, as she stops
scrolling and exhales through her nose. Small frustrated shake of the head. She
mouths a short silent word to herself. No dialogue audible.

Shot 3, three seconds: her hands come back to the keyboard, and she presses two
keys deliberately — one thumb, one wide key. Extreme close-up on the hands and the
keyboard only, screen entirely out of frame. Her shoulders drop half an inch.

Shot 4, three seconds: back to the wider framing. She is typing normally again,
relaxed, faintly amused at herself, still not looking at the camera. Warm daylight,
calm room, empty wall behind her.

Style throughout: shot handheld on an iPhone by a friend, slight tilt and
imperfect framing, natural light only, no flash and no studio lighting. Muted
desaturated palette — off-white walls, charcoal knit sweater, honey-toned wood.
Exactly one small teal object on the desk as the only saturated colour. Fine sensor
grain, real skin texture, unedited phone-camera colour. Realistic, unglamorous,
present-day. No spoken dialogue, no music cues, no captions.

## Wat jij er daarna overheen zet

De video is het moment. De woorden zijn van jou, en die staan al vast in Product
Truth — dus ze kloppen.

| Tijd | Tekst |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Where did I copy that? |
| 0:03 | You had it. It's gone. |
| 0:06 | `⌥Space` |
| 0:09 | Everything you copy or say, one keystroke away. |
| 0:11 | VoxClip · free · Mac & Windows · voxclip.it |

`⌥Space` is de Quick-picker. Niet `⌘⇧Space` — dat start dictation. Zie
`docs/product-truth.md`.

De link staat niet in beeld maar in het bijschrift, getagd, zodat je de klik kunt
meten.

## Daarna

Bestand in Studio → **Assets** → uploaden → bij *Where it came from* kies
**Model-generated**. Dat is geen formaliteit: de Studio weigert gegenereerd beeld
als je het als screenshot opvoert.
